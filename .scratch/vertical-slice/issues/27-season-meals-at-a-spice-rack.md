# Add the element ingredient after cooking, at a spice rack

Status: ready-for-agent
Type: task
Blocked by: 08

## Why
(Ryan, 3 Nov, playtest) Adding the accelerator (element slot) while cooking is too many options at once, and it's harder to see the specific bonus. Do it afterwards, at another workbench.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] The stove only cooks: no element slot there.
- [ ] A spice rack by the cooking area: pick a cooked meal, then an element ingredient; the effect is spelled out large (infuse: your attacks become Fire this run; guard: Frost hits you for half).
- [ ] One element ingredient per meal; it's spent when you season; a seasoned meal's card shows the element (colour tint, ticket 09).
- [ ] Opens with the kitchen (opening arc).
